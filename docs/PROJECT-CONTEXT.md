# CapitolWonk project context

CapitolWonk is Tyler's mobile-first civic intelligence product, targeting a controlled **November 16, 2026 soft launch** followed by the **January 3, 2027 full launch for the 120th Congress**. Public feature language is **Daily Brief**; internal `Weekly Brief` compatibility names remain. The product combines legislative/member information, accountability, saved civic activity, subscriptions, and a native iOS shell around the web app. Tyler is product owner and creative lead; Codex owns technical execution and continuity. The visual direction is premium dark navy, gold, and glassmorphism. Current scope serves the TestFlight/App Store and 120th Congress transition path; the Supreme Court sister app, state-legislation pilot, and Daily Brief outbound delivery are later tracks.

## Operating constraints

Routine source-only work, safe diagnostics, checks, commits, non-destructive pushes, and narrowly scoped low-risk PR work may proceed without a confirmation loop. Major architecture/security, production state or capability, protected configuration, Apple account/signing, provider/destructive, billing/product, uploads/distribution/submission/release actions require Tyler's exact action-time approval; see the full [Standing Rules](eod-handoff-template.md#standing-rules). No approval for one gate authorizes the next.

Keep first-party privacy intake/operations/monitoring/retention/deletion and App Store server processing off until independently evidenced and approved. The dedicated privacy mailbox is the launch intake path; the database-backed operator sequence is conditional post-launch unless Tyler reselects it. Tyler's separately approved T04 WWDR G3 repair, Development profile, local signed archive and build 2 iPhone install/launch were completed September 16; do not repeat that work. Export, TestFlight upload/distribution, App Review and release retain separate exact gates. The November 16 soft launch and January 3 full launch are targets, not release authorization; follow the [transition plan](launch-transition-plan-2026-09-25.md). Keep the in-app browser visible for app QA and useful evidence open; do not close user tabs merely to tidy.

When a new request seems to conflict with a settled decision, cite the prior record and the practical consequence, then distinguish Tyler's intentional change from a misunderstanding. If evidence is absent, say so; do not improvise a completed or tested state.
