## Apple field rules used for this worksheet

- Apple limits the localized app name to 2–30 characters and the subtitle to 30 characters: [App information reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information).
- Apple limits promotional text to 170 characters, description to 4,000 characters, and keywords to 100 bytes; keyword terms must be longer than two characters and should not duplicate the app or company name: [Platform version information reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information/).
- Apple requires a privacy policy URL and treats the User Privacy Choices URL as optional: [App Privacy reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-privacy/).
- Apple currently supports App Store tags in the United States and lets authorized roles review selected tags: [Manage App Tags](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-tags).
- Remote status, localization, editability, and displayed field limits must still be confirmed in the exact App Store Connect record before any proposed change.
