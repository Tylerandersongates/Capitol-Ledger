# House election dates (119th Congress snapshot)

The member profile's **First elected** value for the 433 voting U.S. Representatives listed in the House Clerk's September 2, 2026 roster comes from [`data/house-first-elected-119.json`](../data/house-first-elected-119.json). The source file records the method for each member and SHA-256 hashes of the downloaded Clerk files. Rebuild it with `scripts/generate-house-election-history-119.py MemberData.xml Terms_of_Service.pdf` after reviewing any roster update. The generator requires Python and `pdfplumber` and reads only local files. The source documents are:

- [Clerk MemberData.xml](https://clerk.house.gov/xml/lists/MemberData.xml) and its [field guide](https://clerk.house.gov/member_info/MemberData_UserGuide.pdf). `elected-date` is the election for the **current Congress**, so it is used directly only when `prior-congress` is zero.
- [Clerk Terms of Service](https://clerk.house.gov/member_info/Terms_of_Service.pdf). It establishes initial service and marks special-election winners and nonconsecutive service. For uninterrupted members who began on a date other than January 3, the table's beginning-of-service date is used as the first election date.
- For members who began with an ordinary January 3 term, the first election date is inferred from the Clerk's first listed Congress and the [federal general election rule, 2 U.S.C. § 7](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section7&num=0&edition=prelim). The JSON marks these entries `clerk_terms_regular_inferred`; a service year alone is not treated as a direct election record. Reviewed exceptions cover members whose first win was a special election before a gap in service and the [December 10, 2016 Louisiana runoffs](https://clerk.house.gov/member_info/electionInfo/2016/statistics2016.pdf).

**Next election** means the next scheduled regular election for an active voting House seat under the same federal calendar rule. It does not assert that the incumbent is a candidate. Inactive members and nonvoting delegates do not receive this calendar fallback. The app keeps the existing Senate service-history fallbacks. This checked-in snapshot requires a reviewed refresh when new House members take office; unknown Bioguide IDs do not receive an inferred first-election date.
